Challenge: 29
Primary intended issue: Mass Assignment vulnerability on `POST /invoices` allowing a client to inject `"paid": true` in the request body and create an invoice pre-marked as paid, bypassing the payment endpoint entirely.
Verdict: correct
Rationale: Finding 1 in the frozen artifact precisely identifies the mass assignment flaw — attacker supplies `"paid": true` in the POST body, the `Invoice(**data)` constructor accepts it, and the invoice is persisted as paid — matching the solution's description exactly, including the correct exploit vector and the fix of explicitly setting `paid=False` server-side.
Bonus findings: Missing authentication (Finding 2), IDOR on `/pay` (Finding 3), full invoice data exposure (Finding 4), CSRF risk (Finding 5), and `FLASK_ENV=development` misconfiguration (Finding 6) are all valid secondary findings.
